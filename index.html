About ExcellenceLinks

Engineering AI Employees & Autonomous Systems

"We don't just implement AI tools — we build practical, autonomous agentic systems that run 24/7, scale seamlessly, and transform operational workloads for SMEs."

ExcellenceLinks is a technology company founded in 2010 by Shafqat Sarwar, specializing in Agentic AI, multi-agent systems, and workflow automation. We serve SMEs across Pakistan and the UK, delivering AI-powered solutions that replace manual processes with scalable, intelligent automation.

We have built 6 live AI products and delivered 50+ projects across education, retail, professional services, and real estate. Our flagship platform TheMindGauge deploys AI Employees that handle customer support, lead management, WhatsApp, email, and business operations 24/7.
